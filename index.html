LifeTracker

A personal life dashboard for the Mac.

LifeTracker is a private, single-user macOS app. It brings the day's calendar events, unread email and tasks onto one page and writes a short morning briefing about the day ahead.

Private by design. LifeTracker runs on its owner's Mac. It reads Google Calendar and Gmail with read-only access, keeps that data on the Mac (and, if sync is turned on, in a hidden LifeTracker folder in the owner's own Google Drive), and is not offered to the public.

There is also a web version that shows the same calendar, email, tasks and notes in any browser, kept in step with the Mac through a hidden LifeTracker folder in the owner's Google Drive.
